Unit 12 Relay Trainer

Patch the 120 V power circuit and the 24 V control circuit, then run it.

Cord
Probe: hover a jack to read its voltage.

Drag from one jack to another to patch a cord, or click one jack and then another. Cords stack like banana plugs. Click a cord to select it, then press Delete. Hold a pushbutton to press it; shift-click locks it in.

Circuit

Supply

Relay coils pick up at 85 % of 24 V (20.4 V) and drop out below 55 % (13.2 V).

Ladder diagram

Drawn from the cords on the board: 120 V source and power circuit on top, transformer T1, then the 24 V control circuit. Each new cord adds its part of the circuit; the parts touched by the latest cord are highlighted, and red circles mark open ends.
